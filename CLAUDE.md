# IlmXL

A K-12 learning platform (Pre-K to grade 12): Maths, English, Science, Canadian Social Studies, Coding and Pre-K
Activities, with an on-screen teacher. Static Vite site (no framework), published to GitHub Pages at /ilmxl/ by
`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`. Separate from Sanad (the Qur'an platform); nothing is shared at runtime.

- Hash routes in `src/main.mjs`; views in `src/views/`; logic in `src/app/`.
- Maths: `src/math/skills.mjs`, every question generated by code from a seed, with worked steps and (where possible)
  an `expr` the checker re-computes. Standard North American progression; 9 = Algebra 1, 10 = Geometry,
  11 = Algebra 2, 12 = Precalculus/intro calculus.
  Most skills live in `src/math/more/g0.mjs … g12.mjs` (helpers in `src/math/kit.mjs`): one or more for each category of
  the grade, in the grade's category order; a skill's `strand` is its category. Coverage was checked against the
  maths categories IXL Canada lists for each grade (Nov 2026; every one covered); questions are our own wording.
  `RECAT` in skills.mjs files the original skills under those categories. `ONLY=math-4- npm run check` checks one grade.
- Skills catalogue (`src/views/subject.mjs`): grade rail, lettered categories, numbered skills; maths also by topic
  (`src/app/topics.mjs`, keyword patterns) and the Fluency Zone (`src/views/fluency.mjs`, one-minute fact sprints).
  Both are public: visitors can browse and try a sprint; practising needs an account.
- Written banks: `src/content/{english,science,social}.mjs`, format in `src/content/FORMAT.md`.
  Social Studies is Canadian; skills with `province: 'NS'` are Nova Scotia's (the first province). Others to come.
- Coding puzzles: `src/app/coding.mjs`, each with a known solution.
- Worked steps (`src/math/steps.mjs`): a step is a string or `{ text, say?, visual? }`, one idea per step. Use the
  builders (columnAdd/columnSub with decimals and regrouping, columnMul/columnMul1, longDivision, numberLine,
  fractionBars, eqnLines, areaModel) so the working is drawn as a teacher writes it; `visual()` in `src/app/ui.mjs`
  draws every picture type. `npm run check` fails if a generator's last two steps never reach its answer.
- Geometry figures: `src/math/figs.mjs` (Node-safe builders: labelled polygons with marks, heights and highlights,
  circles, angle diagrams, solids in oblique 3-D with hidden edges dashed, nets, cross-sections) → `{ type: 'fig' }`,
  drawn by `src/app/figure.mjs` (theme colours in the `--fg-*` CSS variables, chalk colours on the classroom board).
  `{ type: 'figs', figs, captions }` shows several side by side. Every geometry question should have its figure, and
  its worked steps should highlight (`hl`) the part each step uses; `src/math/more/geo10.mjs` is the pattern.
  The checker validates every figure (`figProblems`).
- Lessons (`lessonOf` in curriculum.mjs): key ideas (a lesson line may be `{ text, visual }`), two worked examples of
  different kinds, then three check questions (two right completes the lesson).
- Unit exams (`#/exam/<subject>/<grade>/<unit code | all>`, `src/views/exam.mjs`, sets from `src/app/exams.mjs`): 10
  questions per unit, 25 for a grade exam, no marking until the end, then results by skill and every question with
  its working; best/last scores in `progress.exams`. Printable worksheets with answer keys (optionally with worked
  solutions): `#/worksheet/…[/<seed>]`, public. The checker makes every unit's exam.
- Explanations (`src/app/explain.mjs`): the step-by-step player, used after a wrong answer, on "See the steps",
  in "Learn with an example" (practice) and in lessons. Spoken text: `stepSay()`/`speakable()` in curriculum.mjs
  (fractions as "3 quarters", "negative 7", "plus").
- The teacher (`src/app/teacher.mjs`): Ms. Amina / Mr. Yusuf, our own SVG drawings. Voice (`src/app/voice.mjs`):
  Kokoro (open, Apache-2.0) made in the browser (model ~90 MB from Hugging Face, once, cached); af_heart / am_michael.
  A question's lines are prepared while the learner thinks; a sentence not ready in 2.5 s uses the best device voice
  (Edge Natural, Google, Apple Enhanced first). One voice at a time: every say() cuts off the last. Listening:
  browser speech recognition (typing always works instead).

## Checks

`npm run check` (part of `npm run build`) must pass: every maths generator over 400 seeds, every bank item, every
puzzle solution, the answer marker. Never invent facts in the banks; if unsure, leave the item out.

## Accounts

Like Ta'aleem: a Google Apps Script web app on a Google Sheet (`apps-script/Code.gs`, set-up in its README).
Parents/guardians, students 13+ and teachers sign up with a username and password (salted, hashed server-side;
signed 60-day tokens). Children are learners inside an account, by first name and grade only; no email collected.
The site reads the server URL from `VITE_ILMXL_SERVER` (GitHub variable `ILMXL_SERVER_URL`). Without it,
accounts are device-only (PBKDF2 in the browser) and the pages say so. Client: `src/app/account.mjs`; each
account's learners are in localStorage `ilmxl-v1:<username>` and every change is queued to the server
(`store.mjs` save → `queueSave`). Public routes: welcome, signin, signup, about; everything else needs sign-in.

## Design

The user chose a modern product look in the spirit of the 21st.dev / shadcn community components (this replaces
the "no glass, no glow" rule used in Sanad and Ta'aleem; it does not apply here). Tokens and everything else in
`src/styles/ilmxl.css`: zinc neutrals, the indigo → violet → pink brand gradient, Bricolage Grotesque headings,
Plus Jakarta Sans text, Baloo 2 for young learners (`html[data-young="1"]`, Pre-K to grade 3), floating glass navbar,
spotlight cards (`.spot`), aurora/grid backgrounds, shimmer buttons, `[data-reveal]` scroll-ins, light and dark
(`data-theme`). Appearance matters a great deal to the user: check new screens in both themes and at phone width.
Icons are our own SVGs in `src/app/ui.mjs`; no emoji icons. Honour prefers-reduced-motion.

## Privacy

Say accurately that the browser's speech recognition may send audio to the browser maker, and that the natural
voice is made on the device (its model is downloaded from Hugging Face).
