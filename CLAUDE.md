# IlmXL

A K-12 learning platform (Pre-K to grade 12): Maths, English, Science, Canadian Social Studies, Coding and Pre-K
Activities, with an on-screen teacher. Static Vite site (no framework), published to GitHub Pages at /ilmxl/ by
`.github/workflows/deploy.yml`. Separate from Sanad (the Qur'an platform); nothing is shared at runtime.

- Hash routes in `src/main.mjs`; views in `src/views/`; logic in `src/app/`.
- Maths: `src/math/skills.mjs`, every question generated by code from a seed, with worked steps and (where possible)
  an `expr` the checker re-computes. Standard North American progression; 9 = Algebra 1, 10 = Geometry,
  11 = Algebra 2, 12 = Precalculus/intro calculus.
- Written banks: `src/content/{english,science,social}.mjs`, format in `src/content/FORMAT.md`.
  Social Studies is Canadian; skills with `province: 'NS'` are Nova Scotia's (the first province). Others to come.
- Coding puzzles: `src/app/coding.mjs`, each with a known solution.
- The teacher (`src/app/teacher.mjs`): Ms. Amina / Mr. Yusuf, our own SVG drawings; browser speech synthesis and
  recognition (typing always works instead).

## Checks

`npm run check` (part of `npm run build`) must pass: every maths generator over 400 seeds, every bank item, every
puzzle solution, the answer marker. Never invent facts in the banks; if unsure, leave the item out.

## Design

Tokens in `src/styles/ilmxl.css`. Young style (`html[data-young="1"]`, Pre-K to grade 3): bigger type and targets,
a number keypad, questions read aloud. No glass, glow or emoji icons; icons are our own SVGs in `src/app/ui.mjs`.

## Privacy

No accounts, no server: learners and progress in localStorage (`ilmxl-v1`). Say accurately that the browser's
speech recognition may send audio to the browser maker.
