# Sources and alignment

All questions, lessons, drawings and code are original to IlmXL. Curricula used as guides for scope and order:

- **Maths**: the usual North American progression, as set out in the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics
  (K-8 and high-school conceptual categories).
- **English**: Common Core English Language Arts standards (foundational skills, language, reading, writing).
- **Science**: Next Generation Science Standards topic progression (life, physical, Earth and space science).
- **Social Studies**: pan-Canadian topics; Nova Scotia's provincial curriculum outcomes (Department of Education
  and Early Childhood Development) for the Nova Scotia skills. Facts checked against Government of Canada sources
  (canada.ca, Parliament of Canada, Library and Archives Canada) and the Truth and Reconciliation Commission's reports.

Teaching approach: the lesson shape (key idea with a labelled diagram, worked examples where each step
highlights the part being used, check questions, unit tests) follows common textbook practice, as in the Ontario
Principles of Mathematics 10 textbook and the Siyavula Everything Maths Grade 10 book the team reviewed. No text,
questions or diagrams were copied from them (the first is all rights reserved; the second is CC BY-ND, which allows
no adapted versions). Every figure is drawn by our own code (src/math/figs.mjs).

Planned: openly licensed material (CK-12, OpenStax; with attribution and within their licences) for longer lessons,
and further provinces' Social Studies.
